Whisker gives you a layered access system so you can secure your research at exactly the right level of granularity. Whether you need a quick password gate for an external collaborator, structured role-based permissions for your team, or a temporary share link for a stakeholder, Whisker lets you apply the right control without getting in the way of your workflow.

## Two Layers of Access Control

Whisker combines two complementary access models that you can use independently or together:

1. **Password Protection** — Lock any collection or document with a password. Anyone who knows the password can access the resource without needing a Whisker account. This is ideal for lightweight, time-limited external sharing.

2. **Role-Based Access** — Assign specific Whisker users a role on a collection or document. Roles determine exactly what each person can do: **Owner**, **Editor**, or **Viewer**. Role members always bypass password prompts on resources they have been granted access to.

## Access Hierarchy

Whisker applies permissions at three levels, from broadest to most specific:

- **Workspace** — Settings here apply to every collection and document in your workspace by default.
- **Collection** — Settings here apply to all documents inside that collection, overriding workspace-level settings where they conflict.
- **Document** — Settings here apply only to the individual document and take precedence over both collection-level and workspace-level settings.

This means you can set a permissive default at the workspace level and then lock down sensitive collections or individual documents as needed.

<Info>
  More specific settings always override broader ones. A document-level
  permission takes precedence over a collection-level permission, which in
  turn takes precedence over a workspace-level permission. Use this to your
  advantage when you need to share one document from an otherwise private
  collection.
</Info>
