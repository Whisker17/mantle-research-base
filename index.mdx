Welcome to Whisker — the secure knowledge base designed for researchers, analysts, and teams who need to store, organize, and share research content and reports. Whisker gives you granular control over who (and what) can access your content, with first-class support for AI agents so your assistants can read exactly the documents you authorize, nothing more.

## Get up and running in four steps

Whether you're onboarding solo or setting up a workspace for your whole team, Whisker gets you from zero to a fully connected knowledge base quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.whisker.app](https://app.whisker.app) and set up your workspace with your organization name and preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your content">
    Add research notes, reports, PDFs, or markdown files to your workspace — either through the dashboard or via the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set permissions">
    Control who can read or edit each document or collection. Apply password protection or assign role-based access to teammates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    Generate an agent token and hand it to your AI assistant. Your agent can now securely read every document you've authorized.
  </Step>
</Steps>
