Whisker is a secure knowledge base built for storing and organizing research content — reports, notes, PDFs, and markdown documents — with the access controls your work demands. Unlike generic file storage, Whisker is purpose-built for research workflows: you get fine-grained permissions at every level of your content hierarchy, and a first-class integration model that lets AI agents read exactly the documents you authorize, with no extra configuration required.

## Who is Whisker for?

Whisker is designed for anyone who produces or works with research content and wants to keep it organized, secure, and reachable by AI tools.

- **Researchers and academics** who accumulate large bodies of notes, literature reviews, and reports and need a reliable place to store and retrieve them.
- **Analysts and knowledge workers** who produce recurring reports and want controlled sharing with internal stakeholders or automated systems.
- **Teams building AI-powered workflows** who need their assistants to reference authoritative internal documents without exposing the entire knowledge base.
- **Enterprises managing sensitive content** that requires role-based access, audit trails, and password-protected sharing.

## How Whisker works

Whisker organizes your content in three layers, and access is controlled at each one.

1. **Upload and organize** — Add documents to your workspace and group them into collections. Collections act like folders, but with their own permission settings independent of the documents inside them.
2. **Set access rules** — Assign roles to teammates and optionally add password protection to documents or collections. Only users and agents with the right credentials can read protected content.
3. **Connect your tools** — Generate an agent token for your AI assistant and point it at the Whisker API. Your agent retrieves documents as structured data, ready for retrieval-augmented generation or any other downstream use.
