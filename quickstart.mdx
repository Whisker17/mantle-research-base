---
title: "Quickstart: Create Documents and Connect an AI Agent"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Create your Whisker account, upload your first document, set access permissions, and connect an AI agent — all in under 10 minutes."
---

This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to a fully operational knowledge base with an AI agent reading your content. Each step takes only a minute or two, and you'll have a working setup before your coffee gets cold.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.whisker.app](https://app.whisker.app) and sign up with your email address. After verifying your email, you'll be prompted to name your **workspace** — this is the top-level home for all your documents and collections. Choose a name that reflects your organization or project.

    <Tip>
      If your team is already using Whisker, ask your workspace Owner to send you an invite link instead of creating a new workspace.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Collection">
    Collections keep your research organized. From your workspace dashboard, click **New Collection** and give it a descriptive name — for example, "Q3 Market Research" or "Clinical Trial Reports."

    You can nest collections inside each other and set independent access permissions on each one. Start with at least one collection before uploading documents so your content has a home from the start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your first document">
    Open your new collection and upload a document using one of two methods:

    **Drag and drop** — Drag any PDF, markdown file, or plain-text document directly onto the collection page in the dashboard.

    **API upload** — Use the Whisker API to programmatically add documents from your own systems:

    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.whisker.app/v1/documents/upload \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -F "file=@report.pdf" \
      -F "collection_id=col_abc123" \
      -F "title=Q3 Market Research Report"
    ```

    Whisker processes the document and makes it searchable within a few seconds of upload.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set access permissions">
    By default, a new document inherits the permissions of its parent collection. To customize access, open the document and click **Permissions**.

    You have two primary tools:

    - **Role-based access** — Assign teammates as Viewer, Editor, or Owner of the document or collection.
    - **Password protection** — Set a password that any user (or agent) must supply to read the document, regardless of their role.

    You can combine both: require a role *and* a password for your most sensitive content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate an agent token">
    To let an AI agent read your content, you need a scoped agent token. Go to **Settings → Agent Tokens** in your workspace and click **New Token**. Give it a descriptive name (e.g., "Research Assistant – GPT"), select the collections it should be able to access, and click **Generate**.

    Copy the token immediately — Whisker won't show it again.

    Pass the token to your agent as a Bearer header when it calls the Whisker API:

    ```bash
    curl https://api.whisker.app/v1/documents/doc_xyz789 \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_AGENT_TOKEN"
    ```

    The response contains the document content in structured JSON, ready for your agent to consume:

    ```json
    {
      "id": "doc_xyz789",
      "title": "Q3 Market Research Report",
      "content": "...",
      "collection_id": "col_abc123",
      "created_at": "2024-09-01T10:00:00Z"
    }
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep your agent tokens secret. Anyone who holds a token can read every collection it's scoped to. Store tokens in environment variables or a secrets manager — never hard-code them in source files or commit them to version control.
</Note>
