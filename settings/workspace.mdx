Your workspace is the shared environment where your team stores, organizes, and accesses research content. Workspace settings let you control its identity, who belongs to it, and how your subscription is managed. To get there, click your workspace name in the sidebar and select **Workspace Settings**.

<Note>
  Only workspace **Owners** and **Admins** can view and edit workspace settings. Members can view their own profile settings but cannot modify workspace-level configuration.
</Note>

## General Settings

The **General** tab lets you update the basic identity of your workspace.

- **Workspace name** — The display name shown in the sidebar and across the UI. Choose something descriptive that reflects your team or project.
- **Slug** — The URL-friendly identifier used in your workspace's web address (for example, `whisker.app/ws/your-slug`). Changing the slug will update all workspace URLs, so notify your team before making this change.
- **Description** — An optional short summary of your workspace's purpose. This is visible to members on the workspace home screen.

Click **Save Changes** to apply any updates.

## Managing Members

Collaborating with your team starts here. Open the **Members** tab to see everyone currently in your workspace and manage their access.

**Inviting new members:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members tab">
    Go to **Workspace Settings > Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the person's email address, and select the role you want to assign them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The recipient receives an email with a link to join the workspace. The invitation expires after 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Workspace roles:**

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Owner** | Full control, including billing, workspace deletion, and role changes. There is always at least one Owner per workspace. |
| **Admin** | Can manage members, integrations, and settings, but cannot delete the workspace or change the Owner. |
| **Member** | Can read and contribute to content based on collection-level permissions. Cannot access workspace settings. |

**Removing a member:**

Navigate to **Workspace Settings > Members**, find the member in the list, click the **⋯** menu next to their name, and select **Remove from workspace**. Their access is revoked immediately. Content they created remains in the workspace.

## Usage & Limits

The **Usage** tab gives you a real-time view of your workspace's consumption against your plan limits.

- **Storage** — Total size of all documents, attachments, and indexed content.
- **Document count** — The number of documents stored across all collections.
- **API call count** — The number of API requests made during the current billing period, including requests from connected agents and integrations.

When you approach a plan limit, Whisker displays a warning banner. If you exceed a limit, new writes and API calls may be paused until you upgrade or the billing period resets.

## Subscription & Billing

Manage your plan and payment details from the **Billing** tab.

- **Upgrade or downgrade** — Click **Change Plan** to move to a different tier. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the end of the current billing period.
- **Invoices** — View and download past invoices from the **Billing History** section.
- **Payment method** — Click **Update Payment Method** to add or replace a credit card or other supported payment option.

**Available plans:**

| Plan | Documents | Storage | Agent Tokens | Team Members |
|------|-----------|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Free | 100 | 1 GB | 1 | 1 |
| Pro | 10,000 | 50 GB | 10 | 10 |
| Team | Unlimited | 500 GB | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Info>
  Agent tokens represent the number of distinct AI agents or API consumers that can be connected to your workspace simultaneously. If you're running automated pipelines, make sure your plan covers the number of active agents you need.
</Info>
